# Audit, October 2026

Scope: rules and numbers across the site, README, docs, `.env.example`, code defaults, the org profile and the X images; exploits; copy; links; accessibility; mobile; empty and error states. Full lifecycle simulation: `sim/report.md` (66 of 66 checks pass on a local validator).

## Fixed

- The winner was picked with float `sqrt(ui balance)` while payouts and the tally proof use the integer `floor(sqrt(raw))`. They could disagree near a tie, and then the round could not be proven. The winner now uses the same integer weights, with ties going to the earlier ballot entry.
- Balances at close were read one wallet after another, so tokens moved from an already-read wallet to an unread one could count twice. They are now read in parallel batches. Some risk remains, see below.
- Paid replies generated while the account was in dry run were marked "preview" and never posted after going live, so the burns were wasted. Once the account can post, they are now posted.
- Real Neuronpedia output almost always contains emoji, so the filter dropped every draft and the round posted nothing. Emoji are now removed before the filter runs. Other symbols are still rejected.
- Pump-then-quote: on a thin bonding curve, pumping the price for a moment made the nomination burn cheap (248,380 tokens down to 10,000 in the simulation). Quotes now use the lowest price of the last `PRICE_WINDOW_MIN` (30) minutes.
- The burn panels said "about $5" even when the token amount was clamped. They now show the dollar value of the amount actually quoted. The copy uses "$1.50" instead of "$1.5".
- The 100,000 threshold for submitting features was missing on the landing page. It is now added and read from config.
- README: added the supply line (1,000,000,000, fair launch, no presale) to match the tokenomics image, and fixed the filter description.
- `docs/fees-and-burns.md` still called the verifier "future". It now links to `zk.md`.
- /verify:
  - Added clear states for a round that has not closed, a proof still in progress, and a round that is not proven.
  - It now shows the latest proven round by default.
  - The copy command was shown in capitals by CSS, and it said "nothing is sent anywhere", which was wrong.
  - Added to the nav.
- Mobile nav links now have 44 px tap targets on touch screens.
- If a round's on-chain record already exists, it is accepted only when it matches the proof, and otherwise reported as a conflict.
- `zk-verify.js` takes the program and publisher from the site, and both can be pinned with env vars.

## Judgment calls

Update 2026-10-20: items 1 to 5 and 7 below were approved and implemented. Payouts are pro rata by raw balance; each voted round pays 10% of the pool; caps are now 2,000,000 and 600,000 tokens; paid replies only go to posts that mention the account or are by the requester's linked handle; tallies are hidden while a round is open; ZK copy says "in testing". Item 6 (single-slot reads) is still open.


1. **Sybil splitting.** With square-root weight, splitting a balance across k wallets multiplies its weight by sqrt(k). Four wallets give twice the weight, and that also doubles the fee share. This is how square-root weighting works without identity. Options:
   - Linear weight for payouts, keeping square root for steering only.
   - A minimum balance per counted wallet.
   - Accept it and say so.
2. **Pool drains in one round.** Each voted round distributes everything the distributor holds above the reserve (up to the daily cap). The first round after funding takes it all, which rewards timing a single round. A drip, such as 1/48 of the balance per round, would spread it over a day.
3. **Burn caps at launch.** At a launch market cap of about $5k, $5 is about 1M tokens, but `NC_NOMINATE_MAX_TOKENS` is 500,000. The real burn is then worth about $2.50, and the reply burn is affected the same way. The UI now shows the true value. Raise the caps, or accept it.
4. **Paid replies to any post.** The account replies to posts whose authors never mentioned it. X's automation rules restrict unsolicited automated replies, so this risks the account. Suggestion: allow only replies to posts that mention @neurocracyfun, or to the requester's own posts.
5. **Live tallies invite herding and vote buying.** Weights are visible while the round is open, and the fee share pays winning voters, so voting for the leader is rational and bribes are easy to verify (votes are public). Possible fixes: hide tallies until close, or use commit-reveal (see the ZK roadmap).
6. **Balance at close is not one slot.** Parallel reads shrink the window but do not close it. The fix is reading every voter's token accounts in one `getMultipleAccounts` call at one slot.
7. **ZK claims.** The tokenomics image says "Every tally proven with zero knowledge" and the announcement says "Verified on Solana". This is true only once the proving key is hosted (`ZK_ZKEY_URL`), `ZK_PROVE=1` is set and the program is deployed. House rounds and rounds over 128 votes or 32 candidates are never proven. Suggested wording: "Every voted round proven with zero knowledge". "On Solana" should wait for the mainnet deploy.
8. **Symbols in X images.** The images use `$NCRACY` and `100,000+`. The cashtag is standard, and the plus sign could be "100,000 or more".

## Suggested next steps, by priority

1. Decide 4 (X policy) and 1/2 (economics) before turning on payouts and paid replies.
2. Host the proving key, set `ZK_PROVE=1`, and use the Standard plan (Starter has about 80 MB of headroom).
3. Use single-slot balance reads at close (6).
4. Commit-reveal or hidden tallies (5).
5. A multi-party phase 2 ceremony before mainnet relies on proofs.
6. Cut the 680 KB snarkjs bundle on /verify (it loads only on demand now), for example with a smaller verifier-only build.
