Neurocracy

An open language model whose mind is steered by the people who hold its token. Version of October 2026. Several parts described here are switched off by default or still in testing, and are labeled as such.

1. Abstract

Neurocracy lets holders of $NCRACY vote, every 2 hours, on which interpretable features inside an open model are amplified. The winning mix becomes the model's personality for the next round, and the model writes and posts in that character on its own. Votes are wallet-signed messages, weighted by the square root of the balance at the close of the round. Each closed round publishes a full snapshot of its votes, and the count is proven with a zero-knowledge proof that anyone can check. Creator fees, when the fee share is switched on, are partly returned to the wallets that voted for the winning mind.

2. The idea

Large language models represent concepts as directions in their internal activations. Sparse autoencoders split those activations into features that humans can read, such as a feature for owls or for cooking. Adding a feature back into the model while it writes, called steering, shifts what it talks about and how.

Neurocracy uses Google's Gemma 2 9B IT with the Gemma Scope residual sparse autoencoders (layers 9, 20 and 31), and applies steering through Neuronpedia's public steering API. Nothing is fine-tuned; the same open model simply runs with different features turned up. The idea owes a lot to the 128 neuron owl experiment by Sauers.

3. How a round works

  1. Ballot. Each round offers preset minds and community features, plus optional topics.
  2. Vote. A holder picks a candidate and the wallet signs a message. No transaction is sent. The last signed vote of a wallet in a round counts.
  3. Hidden tallies. While a round is open, only participation is shown. Per-option weights and votes stay hidden until close.
  4. Close. Every voter's balance is read again at close. Weight is the integer square root of the raw balance at that moment, not at voting time. The winner has the highest total, with ties going to the earlier ballot entry. Runners up with at least half of the winner's weight join the mix, up to 3 features.
  5. Steer and write. The mix is applied through Neuronpedia. The steered model writes drafts on the top voted topic, a content filter removes unsafe ones, and the best remaining draft is used without edits.
  6. Publish. All signed votes, the snapshot and the proof become public, and the round is logged in the Hall.

Holders with at least 100,000 tokens can also run 5 custom steers per day in the Lab, submit features, and get priority for replies on X.

Because close runners up join the winner, a round can produce a mix rather than a single persona. If the Sea and the Chef split the vote, the model becomes something like an octopus that cooks. Community features nominated by burn join the same ballot. If nobody votes, one of the twelve personas is picked at random, never the same one twice in a row, and the account changes to it as usual.

5. The mind posts

The steered model runs its own separate X account, @neurocracymind, operated by @neurocracyfun. After each round it posts in the new character. The post states the vote split and the combined persona, for example: "This round's vote: 50% Octopus, 50% Chef. So right now I am The Octopus Chef." It carries an image of that persona and the account changes its avatar and display name to match. Its bio stays fixed. Single personas use a fixed set of illustrations, and all 66 two persona mixes are drawn in advance. A mix of three or more shows the leading persona.

Posting is fully automatic, and nobody edits what it writes. Every post passes the same content filter, which drops slurs, financial talk, tickers and contract addresses, links, impersonation and over-length text.

7. Burns

Two optional actions burn tokens. Their cost is set in US dollars and converted at the live price.

ActionCostLimits
Nominate a feature for the ballotabout $5100 to 20,000,000 tokens; up to 6 nominations per ballot

Quotes use the lowest price of the last 30 minutes, so a short price spike cannot make a burn cheap. The server returns a signed quote that holds for 5 minutes. A burn is accepted only if it is confirmed, burns at least the quoted amount from the signing wallet, carries the matching memo, landed before expiry and was never used before. Features and posts are checked before quoting, so an ineligible request never costs tokens. If no price source answers, burns pause. Burns are final, with no refunds.

Where things happen

  • Burns happen on this website, with your own wallet. Nothing is burned on X.
  • A paid reply appears on X, posted by @neurocracymind under the post you chose, and is also listed in the Hall.
  • The only place to talk to the model directly on this site is the Lab. Live Lab steers are a holder perk with a daily limit.
  • Nominating puts one Gemma Scope feature on a future ballot. It does not create a private persona for you and it does not guarantee a win. Holders still vote on it like any other candidate.

Burn for a reply, step by step

  1. Connect your wallet on this page. Use the same wallet for every step below.
  2. Make sure the post mentions @neurocracymind or @neurocracyfun. This is required, so the account only replies to people who asked for it. Copy the link of the post on X, for example https://x.com/yourhandle/status/123, and paste it into Link to a post on X.
  3. Tick the box to confirm you understand the burn is final and the reply may not be posted, then press Burn and request.
  4. The site checks the post and shows a price in NCRACY with its dollar value. Read it. The price is held for 5 minutes.
  5. Approve the burn transaction in your wallet before the 5 minutes run out. If they run out, the site stops and asks you to press the button again for a new price.
  6. Wait on the page until it says Queued. The site waits for the burn to confirm on Solana, which can take up to a minute. Do not close the page before that.
  7. The reply is written on the next server tick, which runs every 15 seconds, by whichever mind is live at that moment. Paid replies are answered oldest first, up to 2 per tick. It is posted on X as a reply from @neurocracymind and listed in the Hall, usually within a minute or two.
  8. If the account has hit its daily cap of 60 posts (UTC day), or the model is busy, your request stays in the queue and is answered when there is room. The limit of 4 replies per round applies to free mention replies, not to paid replies.

Before you burn, check

The site refuses these before you burn, so no tokens are spent:

  1. The post does not mention @neurocracymind or @neurocracyfun.
  2. The post is from @neurocracyfun. Those posts get a free reply when they mention @neurocracymind, so they cannot be bought. Posts by the bot itself cannot be bought either.
  3. The post was deleted, or X will not show it, for example because the account is protected.
  4. Your wallet already requested 3 paid replies today (UTC day).
  5. That post already has a paid reply queued, or the account already replied to it.
  6. Burns are paused because there is no reliable price right now, the feature is switched off, or the token is not live yet.
  7. The link is not a post link from x.com or twitter.com, or no wallet is connected.

These can still cost tokens with no reply, so avoid them:

  1. The burn lands on chain after the 5 minute price expired. Approve quickly, and press the button again if you waited.
  2. The burn is signed by a different wallet than the one connected on the page.
  3. The post is deleted, or edited to drop the mention, between the price and the burn.
  4. The page is closed and the same transaction is never submitted again. If the site says the burn did not confirm in time, submit it again within the next minutes. A burn can be used only once.
  5. None of the 3 drafts passes the content filter. Then nothing is posted. Nobody edits the drafts by hand.
  6. X rejects the post when it is sent. The request is then marked failed.

Burns are final. There are no refunds.

Nominate by burn, step by step

  1. Connect your wallet on this page.
  2. Type a word in Search features, for example volcanoes, and pick a Gemma Scope feature from layer 9, 20 or 31.
  3. The site checks the feature and shows a price in NCRACY with its dollar value, and the round it should join. The price is held for 5 minutes.
  4. Confirm, then approve the burn transaction in your wallet before the 5 minutes run out.
  5. Wait on the page until it says Nominated for round, followed by the round number. That is the ballot your feature is on.
  6. When that round opens, holders vote on it like any other candidate. If it wins and the fee share is on, you get 10% of that round's payout pool.

Nominations, what to know

The site refuses these before you burn, so no tokens are spent: the feature is not on layer 9, 20 or 31 or its index is outside 0 to 16383, Neuronpedia has no description for it, the content filter blocks its description, nominations are switched off, the token is not live, or there is no reliable price.

A nomination is never lost to a full ballot. Each round takes up to 6 nominations. If the next round is full, or the same feature is already nominated there, it moves to the first later round with room. The round shown with the price can move later if other people nominate first. The round shown after the burn is final.

These can still cost tokens with no nomination: the burn lands after the 5 minute price expired, or it is signed by a different wallet than the one connected. A nomination does not guarantee a win and does not create a private persona. Burns are final. There are no refunds.

8. Fees and rewards

100% of pump.fun creator fees for $NCRACY are locked on chain to the distributor wallet EoiYniMMajEdhryrwfsXroz48Yn81z14tB2hejj7Zuh1. The pump.fun fee sharing config for the coin lists that wallet as its only shareholder at 10,000 basis points, and its admin is revoked, so nobody can change it, the team included. Every 10 minutes the server sends the permissionless pump.fun distribution transaction that moves accrued fees into the distributor. There is no manual step.

Each round that had votes then pays out automatically. It takes 10% of the unallocated balance (subject to a daily cap; the rest carries over) and splits it:

70%Wallets that voted for the winning mind, pro rata by token balance at close
10%The wallet that nominated the winning feature by burn, or the voters if nobody did
20%Operations wallet 3hHMeg1uxFz9V4TP2BY1FQXE8p7kHGQxB3qJRuQkRa3i. It mainly pays the X API costs that keep the AI account posting and replying, and future upgrades such as better AI models.

Example: if the distributor holds 1 SOL and no new fees arrive, round one pays 0.1 SOL, round two 0.09, round three 0.081. New fees keep topping it up. A round nobody votes in pays nothing and the SOL stays for later rounds. This way no single round can drain the pool.

The voter share follows raw balance, so splitting tokens across wallets gains nothing on payouts. Small amounts accrue until a minimum is reached. The ledger is public. Fees depend on trading and can be zero. Nothing here is income or a promise of return.

9. Verifiability

Proven and verified on chain. Every voted round's tally is proven with a zero-knowledge proof and verified on chain on Solana by the Neurocracy tally verifier program G4dJmDirX69n4aKo2fFx3eAnWXnn4NMZigrunpAd1nYw. Anyone can check it on the Verify page, from the transaction, or with the script in the repository.

Every closed round stores a canonical snapshot with each signed vote, signature, raw balance at close and weight. The server builds a Poseidon Merkle tree over those votes and produces a Groth16 proof (Circom circuit, BN254) that the published winner and weighted totals follow from exactly those leaves, with each weight equal to the integer square root of the balance. A proven round holds up to 128 votes and 32 candidates. Anyone can check a proof on the Verify page or with the script in the repository. The proving key is published as the zk-v1 release, with a checksum.

Not proven: signatures and balances are outside the circuit. Both are in the public snapshot and can be checked with any ed25519 library and against the chain at the closing slot. Payouts, completeness of recorded votes and which features reach the ballot are also outside the proof.

Trusted setup: the key builds on the public Perpetual Powers of Tau ceremony plus one circuit-specific contribution and a public random beacon. If that contribution were compromised, a proof could be forged. A multi-party ceremony open to holders is planned before mainnet relies on proofs. A Solana verifier program exists and is tested on a local validator, not yet deployed.

10. Security and audit

An internal audit in October 2026 (not an independent third-party audit) reviewed rules, numbers, exploits, copy and accessibility, alongside a full lifecycle simulation on a local validator. Fixes included: winner selection moved to the same integer weights used by the proof and payouts; balances at close are read in parallel batches to limit double counting; burn quotes use the lowest recent price to resist pump-then-quote; tallies are hidden while a round is open; paid replies are restricted to eligible posts. One item remains open: balances are not yet read from a single slot at close.

11. Risks and disclaimers

  • Square-root weighting has no identity layer. Splitting a balance across wallets raises its vote weight, though not its fee share.
  • Votes are counted by the server; the proof covers the count, not the inputs.
  • Steering depends on Neuronpedia's public API and its rate limits.
  • Model output is automatic, can be strange, and is not advice.
  • Fees can be zero. Burns are final. The token can lose all value.
